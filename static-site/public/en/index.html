About

RedNet is a CTF team from Kazakhstan. The team came together at Satbayev University and was part of the Satbayev University team.

The team was most active in 2022–2024. According to CTFtime, it took part in 32 CTFs in 2022–2026 and has 30+ writeups.

These days the team competes only occasionally.
